Explore the Mandelbrot set and related fractals directly on your Android device.

Pan, pinch, and double-tap to navigate; long-press to read complex coordinates.
A bottom bar offers zoom, reset, export, and a menu for **12 formulas** (Mandelbrot,
Burning Ship, Tricorn, Julia, Phoenix, and more) and **12 palettes** with preview
swatches. Toggle smooth coloring, set fixed / zoom-scaled / adaptive iteration
limits, and edit Julia or Phoenix parameters. Your last viewport and choices are
restored when you reopen the app. Rendering is progressive and entirely on-device.
The fractal draws edge-to-edge under the system bars.

The app contains no ads, trackers, analytics, network access, or data collection.
